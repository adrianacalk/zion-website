About

Zion Lutheran Cemetery

Zion's cemetery sits on US 90 on the west side of Castroville, off Alsace Avenue, next to St. Louis Cemetery. It has two sections, old and new, and is still in use. Marked graves date to the 1860s and carry names like Wurzbach, Bippert, Haass, Etter, Fuos and Santleben.

For burial plots or questions, contact the church office. Gifts to the Zion Lutheran Cemetery Fund help care for the grounds.
