Ham Dinner Sunday

Zion's biggest day of the year. Worship at 10 am, then stay all day for our annual German-style Ham Dinner, a tradition for many of the years since Zion was founded in 1852.

Next Ham Dinner: October 2027 (date coming soon)

At a glance

  • Worship10 am
  • Serving11 am to 1 pm, or until the food sells out
  • Price$12 a plate
  • Dine in or drive throughEat on the church grounds, or pick up to-go plates at the drive-through
  • PaymentCash or card
  • Pre-sale ticketsAt the church office

The menu

Homemade, traditional German-style: ham, potato salad, coleslaw, beans, bread, dessert and iced tea.
