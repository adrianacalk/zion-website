Rummage Sale

Our Rummage Sale serves Castroville and the surrounding area, in the building across London Street from the church at Fiorella Street.

Open through September up to the Ham Dinner, and open Saturday and Sunday of Ham Dinner weekend. Shopping days are posted on our Facebook page.

Donation guidelines

2026 update: We've paused most donations. Contact the office before dropping anything off.

We gladly accept

  • Gently used clothing (no stains or tears)
  • Working electronics
  • Complete toys and games
  • Clean home decor

We can't accept

  • Broken items
  • Items with missing parts
  • Stained fabrics
  • Heavy older electronics

If you wouldn't give it to a friend, please don't donate it.
