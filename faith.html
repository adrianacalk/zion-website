About

What We Believe

Our mission

We unite to bear witness to and accept God's love through Jesus Christ, by worship, fellowship, education, and service.

Our statement of faith

  1. We believe in the Triune God, the Gospel of Jesus Christ, the Canonical Scriptures of the Old and New Testaments, and God's creative, sustaining and continuing spirit and love working in all creation.
  2. We accept the Apostles', Nicene, and Athanasian Creeds as true declarations of our faith.
  3. We accept the unaltered Augsburg Confession and other writings of the Book of Concord as truthful witnesses and interpretations of our faith.
  4. We believe that by teaching, preaching and living according to these scriptures, creeds and doctrines, and by administering the sacraments, we are a true church, abiding in faith and trusting in God.
