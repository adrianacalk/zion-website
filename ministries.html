Choirs

Our vocal choir and bell choir are led by music director Pete Martinez. They rehearse one evening a week, September to May, and sing and ring on Sundays and holidays. New voices welcome with no experience needed. Call the office to talk with Pete. The calendar shows practice times and performance dates.

Facilities

Our campus on Fiorella Street has the 1939 brick church, the Fellowship Hall/annex, the church office in the Fuos Haus, the youth building which has been hosting the Rummage Sale. The Prayer Garden, planted in 2002 for our 150th anniversary, is open to everyone, all year.

The Fellowship Hall is available for rent by members and the community. Call the office for details.

Cemetery

Zion Lutheran Cemetery is on US 90 on the west side of Castroville, off Alsace Avenue, next to St. Louis Cemetery. It is still in use, and its oldest marked graves date to the 1860s.

Each year on the first Sunday in November we light luminaries there for All Saints Day. Volunteers are welcome to help fill the bags and light the candles.

For burial plots or questions, call the office.
