Parking

Free parking is available in the lot behind the sanctuary and along London and Fiorella Streets. Handicap parking is available right in front of the sanctuary.

Worship style

Our Sunday service is traditional and liturgical, with Holy Communion every week. We mix in contemporary music with traditional hymns on a monthly basis. The bulletins have everything you need to follow along and our choir and bell choir add music through the year. Those who aren't able to receive Communion are still welcome to participate in the service and receive a blessing. Ushers can help with any questions.

Children and youth

Children are welcome in worship. During the service, Myra Hunsicker gathers the children for “Bible Stories with Oma.”

Before worship, Sunday School starts at 8:45 am with praise music in the sanctuary, then classes from 8:45 to 9:45 am (September to May).

The Fellowship Hall is available during the service for babies and toddlers. There is a changing table in the main restroom in the Fellowship Hall.
